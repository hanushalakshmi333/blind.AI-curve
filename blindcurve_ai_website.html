The solution

One curve. Two directions.
Smarter warnings.

The concept combines vehicle detection, local decision-making, and roadside lighting to alert drivers approaching a blind bend.

◉

AI vehicle detection

Computer vision identifies vehicles in camera footage and estimates whether traffic is approaching the blind curve.

↔

Curve-side communication

Detection units on both sides share a simple alert state so drivers in either direction can be warned.

✦

Smart warning lighting

LED indicators can change color or flash when a vehicle is detected, making the hazard visible before the curve.

System workflow

From detection to warning

1
Capture

A roadside camera or sensor observes the approach to the curve.

2
Analyze

An AI model detects vehicles and checks the configured danger zone.

3
Share alert

Edge devices or a communication link pass the detection status to the opposite side.

4
Warn drivers

Smart roadside LEDs activate; the system returns to standby when the road is clear.
